## 🏛️ C1-C2 İleri Sözdizimi ve Vurgu Mimarisi

Üst düzey İngilizcede gramer, sadece "doğru cümle kurmak" için değil, **okuyucunun dikkatini yönlendirmek, ritim oluşturmak ve argümanın ağırlığını artırmak** için bir retorik araç olarak kullanılır.

### 1.1 Inversion (Devrik Yapılar — Tam Kapsamlı Rehber)

**Mantığı:** Cümlenin başına olumsuz, kısıtlayıcı veya koşul bildiren bir ifade getirildiğinde, ana cümle **soru dizilimine** (`Yardımcı Fiil + Özne + Ana Fiil`) geçer. Bu yapı cümleye yüksek düzeyde **resmiyet, edebi güç ve dramatik vurgu** katar.

#### A) Olumsuz ve Kısıtlayıcı Zarflarla Inversion
Formül: **[Olumsuz Zarf] + [Yardımcı Fiil (do/does/did/have/had/should/can/will)] + [Özne] + [Fiil]**

| Başlangıç İfadesi | Anlamı | B2 (Standart Dizilim) | C1-C2 (Inversion ile Vurgulu Dizilim) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Never (before)** | Daha önce asla | *We have never witnessed such rapid inflation.* | ***Never before have we witnessed** such rapid inflation.* |
| **Rarely / Seldom** | Nadiren | *Policy makers rarely consider long-term ecological costs.* | ***Rarely do policy makers consider** long-term ecological costs.* |
| **Hardly / Scarcely ... when** | Tam ... olmuştu ki | *The product had hardly launched when a critical bug emerged.* | ***Hardly had the product launched when** a critical bug emerged.* |
| **No sooner ... than** | ... olmasıyla ... olması bir oldu | *We had no sooner signed the treaty than tensions escalated.* | ***No sooner had we signed** the treaty **than** tensions escalated.* |
| **Little** | Hiç (farkında değildi/bilmiyordu) | *The board knew little about the impending liquidity crisis.* | ***Little did the board know** about the impending liquidity crisis.* |
| **Not only ... but also** | Sadece ... değil, aynı zamanda | *The algorithm reduces latency and optimizes memory usage.* | ***Not only does the algorithm reduce** latency, **but it also** optimizes memory usage.* |
| **Under no circumstances** | Hiçbir koşulda | *Confidential data must not be shared under any circumstances.* | ***Under no circumstances must** confidential data be shared.* |
| **On no account** | Hiçbir suretle / asla | *Researchers should on no account falsify raw metrics.* | ***On no account should researchers** falsify raw metrics.* |
| **At no time** | Hiçbir zaman / hiçbir aşamada | *The defendant was at no time coerced into confessing.* | ***At no time was the defendant** coerced into confessing.* |
| **In no way** | Hiçbir şekilde | *This correlation proves causation in no way.* | ***In no way does this correlation** prove causation.* |

#### B) "Only" ile Başlayan İfadelerde Inversion (Dikkat: İkinci Cümlecik Devrilir!)
`Only after`, `Only when`, `Only by`, `Only if` gibi yapılarda **ilk yan cümlecik normal kalır, ana cümlecik (ikinci kısım) devrik olur**:

* ***Only after** the longitudinal study was completed **did the researchers realize** the magnitude of the anomaly.*
  *(Ancak uzun süreli çalışma tamamlandıktan sonradır ki araştırmacılar anomalinin büyüklüğünü fark etti.)*
* ***Only by** restructuring the supply chain **can the corporation maintain** its competitive edge.*
  *(Şirket rekabet avantajını ancak tedarik zincirini yeniden yapılandırarak koruyabilir.)*
* ***Not until** the late 20th century **did neuroscientists discover** adult neurogenesis.*
  *(Nörobilimciler yetişkin nörogenezini ancak 20. yüzyılın sonlarında keşfettiler.)*

#### C) Koşul Cümlelerinde (Conditionals) "If"siz Inversion
Akademik ve hukuki metinlerde `if` bağlacı atılarak yardımcı fiil (`Should`, `Were`, `Had`) başa çekilir:

1. **Type 1 (Should + Özne + V1):** *If you need further clarification...* ➡️ ***Should you require** further clarification, please do not hesitate to contact the committee.*
2. **Type 2 (Were + Özne + to V1 / Sıfat):** *If the central bank raised interest rates...* ➡️ ***Were the central bank to raise** interest rates prematurely, investment would stagnate.*
3. **Type 3 (Had + Özne + V3):** *If the audit had been conducted earlier...* ➡️ ***Had the audit been conducted** earlier, the insolvency could have been averted.*

#### D) "So / Such ... that" Yapılarında Inversion
* *The market volatility was so severe that investors fled to safe-haven assets.*
  ➡️ ***So severe was the market volatility that** investors fled to safe-haven assets.*
* *The public backlash was such that the legislation was immediately withdrawn.*
  ➡️ ***Such was the public backlash that** the legislation was immediately withdrawn.*

### 1.2 Cleft Sentences (Odaklama ve Vurgu Cümleleri)

Cleft (bölünmüş) cümleler, tek bir önermeyi ikiye bölerek okuyucunun dikkatini **tam olarak vurgulamak istediğiniz bilgiye** (özne, nesne, zaman veya neden) kilitler.

| Cleft Türü | Yapı Kalıbı | B1-B2 Düz Cümle | C1-C2 Cleft (Odaklanmış) Cümle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **It-Cleft** | `It is/was + [Vurgulanan Öğe] + that/who + [Kalan Cümle]` | *Institutional corruption undermined the reform.* | ***It was institutional corruption that** undermined the reform.* |
| **Wh-Cleft (Pseudo-Cleft)** | `What + [Fiil Grubu] + is/was + [Vurgulanan Öğe]` | *We urgently need a paradigm shift in energy policy.* | ***What we urgently need is** a paradigm shift in energy policy.* |
| **All-Cleft** | `All (that) + [Özne + Fiil] + is/was + [Vurgulanan Öğe]` | *The protocol only requires two-factor authentication.* | ***All that the protocol requires is** two-factor authentication.* |
| **Inferential Cleft** | `It is not that ... but rather that ...` | *We don't lack data; we interpret it poorly.* | ***It is not that** we lack empirical data, **but rather that** our analytical framework is flawed.* |
| **The reason why / The thing that** | `The primary reason why ... is that ...` | *Retention dropped because onboarding was complex.* | ***The primary reason why** user retention plummeted **is that** the onboarding flow remained overly convoluted.* |

### 1.3 Participle Clauses (Ortaç Yancümlecikleri)

Akademik yazıda arka arkaya `Because...`, `When...`, `After...`, `Which...` kullanmak metni hantal ve çocuksu gösterir. **Participle Clauses**, aynı özneyi paylaşan iki cümleyi bağlaçsız ve öznesiz birleştirerek **yoğun ve akıcı** bir üslup yaratır.

1. **Present Participle (`V-ing`) — Eşzamanlılık, Sebep veya Sonuç:**
   * **Sebep:** ***Recognizing** the limitations of conventional models, the authors proposed a hybrid architecture.* (`Because they recognized...` yerine)
   * **Sonuç:** *The central bank tightened monetary policy, **triggering** a temporary contraction in credit markets.* (`...which triggered...` yerine)
2. **Past Participle (`V3`) — Edilgen (Passive) Durum veya Koşul:**
   * ***Viewed** from a macroeconomic perspective, the subsidy appears counterproductive.* (`If it is viewed...` yerine)
   * ***Originally designed** for military encryption, the protocol now underpins modern e-commerce.* (`Although it was originally designed...` yerine)
3. **Perfect Participle (`Having + V3` / `Having been + V3`) — Öncelik-Sonralık İlişkisi:**
   * ***Having exhausted** all diplomatic channels, the coalition imposed economic sanctions.* (`After they had exhausted...` yerine)
   * ***Having been validated** across three independent cohorts, the biomarker is now ready for clinical trials.* (`Because it had been validated...` yerine)

> ⚠️ **C1-C2 Altın Kuralı (Dangling Participle Hatası):** Ortaç cümleciğinin gizli öznesi ile virgülden sonraki ana cümlenin öznesi **birebir aynı** olmalıdır!
> * ❌ **Yanlış:** *Walking through the laboratory, the samples were contaminated.* (Laboratuvarda yürüyen örnekler mi? Hayır!)
> * ✅ **Doğru:** *Walking through the laboratory, **the technician** noticed that the samples were contaminated.*

### 1.4 Subjunctive Mood (Dilek/Şart ve Resmi Gereklilik Kipi)

Üst düzey resmi yazışmalarda, hukuk metinlerinde ve akademik önerilerde **Present Subjunctive** kullanılır. Kural çok basittir ama B2 öğrencileri tarafından sıkça unutulur: `that` bağlacından sonra gelen fiil, özne tekil (`he/she/it`) veya geçmiş zaman olsa bile **çekimlenmez (yalın halde / bare infinitive kalır)**; olumsuzda `not + V1`, edilgende `be + V3` kullanılır.

* **Tetikleyici Fiiller:** *recommend, suggest, insist, demand, stipulate, propose, mandate, urge, request*
* **Tetikleyici Sıfatlar:** *imperative, essential, crucial, vital, paramount, mandatory, advisable, necessary*

**Örnekler:**
* *The audit committee **mandated that** the chief financial officer **disclose** all off-balance-sheet liabilities immediately.* (`discloses` veya `disclosed` değil, **disclose**)
* *It is **imperative that** the control group **not be exposed** to external stimuli during the trial.* (`is not exposed` değil, **not be exposed**)
* *The regulatory board **insisted that** every autonomous vehicle **undergo** rigorous stress-testing prior to deployment.*

**Kalıplaşmış (Formulaic) Subjunctive İfadeler:**
* ***Be that as it may**, the core hypothesis remains valid.* (Durum öyle olsa bile / her halükârda, ana hipotez geçerliliğini koruyor.)
* ***Suffice it to say that** the initial projections were overly optimistic.* (Şunu söylemek yeterli olsun ki, ilk tahminler fazlasıyla iyimserdi.)
* ***Come what may**, the institution must uphold academic integrity.* (Ne olursa olsun...)
* ***Far be it from me to** criticize the methodology, yet several variables remain unaccounted for.* (Metodolojiyi eleştirmek haddime değil ama...)

## 🧬 Nominalization (İsimleştirme ve Akademik Yoğunluk)

**Nominalization**, fiilleri (*verbs*) veya sıfatları (*adjectives*) **isim öbeklerine (*noun phrases*)** dönüştürme sanatıdır. Konuşma dili **fiil odaklıdır** (*insanlar bir şey yapar*); akademik ve kurumsal yazı dili ise **kavram ve süreç odaklıdır** (*olgular birbirini etkiler*).

### Neden Nominalization Kullanılır?
1. **Kişisellikten Arındırma (Objectivity):** `We analyzed the data and found that...` yerine `An analysis of the data revealed...` diyerek öznelliği kaldırır.
2. **Bilgi Yoğunluğu (Lexical Density):** Uzun yan cümlecikleri tek bir özne veya nesne grubuna sıkıştırır.
3. **Akademik Akış (Thematic Progression):** Bir cümlenin sonundaki fiili, bir sonraki cümlenin başında isimleştirerek (*"This rapid expansion..."*) kusursuz bir paragraf zinciri kurar.

### Adım Adım Nominalization Dönüşüm Tablosu

| B1-B2: Fiil ve Özne Odaklı (Sözlü/Günlük Stil) | C1-C2: Nominalization (Akademik/Rapor Stili) | Neler Değişti? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *Because cities grew very fast, crime increased.* | ***The rapid proliferation of urban centers** precipitated **an escalation in crime rates**.* | `grew fast` ➡️ *rapid proliferation*; `Because` ➡️ *precipitated*; `crime increased` ➡️ *an escalation in crime rates*. |
| *People are using AI more, so jobs are changing quickly.* | ***The widespread adoption of artificial intelligence** has accelerated **the transformation of the labor market**.* | `using more` ➡️ *widespread adoption*; `so` ➡️ *has accelerated*; `jobs are changing` ➡️ *transformation of the labor market*. |
| *We looked at how employees reacted when salaries were cut.* | ***An assessment of employee reactions to wage reductions** was conducted.* | `We looked at` ➡️ *An assessment of ... was conducted*; `salaries were cut` ➡️ *wage reductions*. |
| *Because the government did not regulate the banks properly, the economy collapsed.* | ***Inadequate regulatory oversight of the banking sector** culminated in **macroeconomic collapse**.* | `did not regulate properly` ➡️ *Inadequate regulatory oversight*; `Because` ➡️ *culminated in*. |
| *It is hard to implement this policy because departments do not communicate well.* | ***The implementation of this policy** is impeded by **interdepartmental communication deficits**.* | `implement` ➡️ *implementation*; `is hard because` ➡️ *is impeded by*; `do not communicate well` ➡️ *communication deficits*. |

> 💡 **Denge Uyarısı ("Zombie Nouns" Tuzağı):** Nominalization'ı aşırıya kaçırıp cümledeki tüm güçlü fiilleri öldürerek sadece `is/was/occurred` bırakmak metni okunmaz hale getirir (*"The implementation of the optimization of the system was carried out by..."*). **En iyi C1-C2 cümlesi:** Güçlü bir isim öbeğini (**Özne**) + Güçlü bir akademik fiille (**Precipitate, Foster, Undermine, Exacerbate**) + Güçlü bir isim öbeğine (**Nesne**) bağlayan cümledir!

## 🛡️ Hedging & Boosting (İhtiyatlı Dil ve Vurgu Dengesi)

Akademik dünyada ve üst düzey danışmanlık raporlarında **mutlak ifadeler (`always`, `never`, `definitely proves`, `everyone knows`) amatörlük işaretidir**. Bilimsel bir makalede veya strateji raporunda hakemlerin/yöneticilerin ilk saldıracağı yer aşırı genellemelerdir.

* **Hedging (İhtiyatlı Dil):** İddianızı eldeki kanıtın gücüyle sınırlandırarak sizi eleştirilere karşı zırhlandıran akademik inceliktir.
* **Boosting (Güçlendirme):** Gerçekten su götürmez bir veri veya konsensüs olduğunda argümanın ağırlığını vurgulayan yapılardır.

### C1-C2 Hedging Araç Seti (5 Kategori)

| Kategori | Kalıplar ve Kelimeler | Akademik Örnek Cümle |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Modal Verbs (Olasılık Modalları)** | *may, might, could, would appear to, cannot be ruled out* | *These anomalies **may** stem from sampling bias rather than a systemic flaw.* |
| **2. Epistemic Verbs (Görünüş ve Çıkarım Fiilleri)** | *appear to, seem to, tend to, suggest, indicate, point toward, imply* | *Preliminary empirical data **tend to indicate** an inverse relationship between the two variables.* |
| **3. Probability Adjectives & Adverbs (Olasılık Sıfat/Zarfları)** | *plausible, probable, conceivable, arguably,presumably, ostensibly, seemingly, potentially, largely, relatively* | *This shift is **arguably** the most significant regulatory overhaul in a decade.* |
| **4. Quantifiers & Frequency (Miktar ve Sıklık Kısıtlayıcılar)** | *a substantial proportion of, in certain respects, to a large extent, under specific conditions* | ***To a large extent**, the model's accuracy hinges on data preprocessing quality.* |
| **5. Impersonal / Passive Framing (Nesnel Çerçeveleme)** | *It is widely held that..., It could be inferred that..., There is grounds to believe that...* | ***It could reasonably be inferred that** consumer confidence will remain subdued in Q3.* |

### Hedging vs. Boosting Karşılaştırması

* ❌ **B1 (Aşırı Kesin / Savunmasız):** *Remote work makes employees more productive.*
* 🛡️ **C1 Hedging (Kanıta Dayalı İhtiyat):** *The findings **seem to suggest** that remote work arrangements **may contribute to** enhanced productivity **among knowledge workers under autonomous management**.*
* 🚀 **C1 Boosting (Güçlü Konsensüs Vurgusu):** *The longitudinal data **unequivocally demonstrate** that chronic sleep deprivation **severely impairs** cognitive executive function.*

## 📄 Akademik Makale, Kurumsal Rapor ve Dokümantasyon Şablonları

### 4.1 IMRaD Akademik Makale İskeleti ve Kalıpları

Uluslararası dergilerde (*IEEE, Nature, Elsevier, Springer*) makale yazarken her bölümün kendine has bir **Phrasebank (Kalıp Bankası)** standardı vardır:

#### 1. Introduction (Giriş — "CARS Modeli": Alan Kurma, Boşluk Bulma, Boşluğu Doldurma)
* **Konunun Önemini Vurgulama:**
  * *"In recent years, there has been a surge of interest in the role of [X] within the domain of [Y]."*
  * *"[X] has emerged as a pivotal factor in understanding how [Y] operates under [Z] conditions."*
* **Literatürdeki Boşluğu (Research Gap) Gösterme:**
  * *"Despite a growing body of literature on [X], remarkably little attention has been paid to [Y]."*
  * *"However, prior studies have predominantly focused on [X], leaving the question of [Y] largely unexplored."*
  * *"A major limitation of existing frameworks lies in their inability to account for [Z]."*
* **Çalışmanın Amacını ve Katkısını İlan Etme:**
  * *"The primary objective of this paper is to bridge this gap by proposing a novel framework for..."*
  * *"To the best of our knowledge, this study represents the first empirical investigation into..."*

#### 2. Methodology (Yöntem ve Veri Toplama)
* **Tasarımı Açıklama:**
  * *"To evaluate the proposed hypothesis, a quantitative/qualitative longitudinal study was conducted across..."*
  * *"Data were harvested from [Kaynak] over a twelve-month period, yielding a sample size of $N = 1,450$."* *(Not: Akademik İngilizcede `data` çoğul kabul edilip `data were` kullanımı çok yaygındır).*
* **Yöntemi Gerekçelendirme:**
  * *"This methodological approach was selected on the grounds that it mitigates confounding variables while preserving..."*

#### 3. Results & Discussion (Bulgular ve Tartışma)
* **Bulguları Sunma:**
  * *"As illustrated in Table 2, there is a statistically significant correlation between [X] and [Y] ($p < 0.01$)."*
  * *"Contrary to initial expectations, the intervention did not yield a marked improvement in..."*
* **Bulguları Yorumlama:**
  * *"These findings corroborate the theoretical predictions put forward by Smith et al. (2023), while challenging the conventional assumption that..."*
  * *"One plausible explanation for this discrepancy is that..."*
* **Kısıtlılıklar ve Gelecek Çalışmalar (Limitations & Future Work):**
  * *"Notwithstanding its contributions, this study is subject to several limitations that warrant consideration."*
  * *"Future research avenues should explore whether these findings generalize to broader demographic cohorts."*

### 4.2 Executive Summary (Yönetici Özeti) ve Rapor Yazımı

Üst düzey yöneticiler (C-Level) 50 sayfalık raporu okumaz; ilk sayfadaki **Executive Summary** bölümüne bakarak karar verirler. Bu bölüm **BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front — Sonuç En Başta)** tekniğiyle yazılır:

```markdown
# EXECUTIVE SUMMARY: Q4 Cloud Infrastructure Optimization

## 1. Context & Problem Statement (Bağlam ve Sorun Tanımı)
Over the past two fiscal quarters, cloud computing expenditures have escalated by 34%, outpacing user growth by a factor of two. An internal audit revealed that 42% of provisioned compute instances remain underutilized during off-peak hours.

## 2. Key Findings (Temel Bulgular)
* **Resource Over-provisioning:** Legacy microservices lack dynamic auto-scaling policies, resulting in $180K of annualized waste.
* **Data Egress Bottlenecks:** Unoptimized cross-region data transfers account for 28% of total bandwidth costs.

## 3. Strategic Recommendations (Stratejik Öneriler)
It is recommended that the Engineering Division execute a two-phase migration toward serverless containers and implement automated spot-instance scheduling by Q2.

## 4. Projected Impact & ROI (Beklenen Etki ve Yatırım Getirisi)
Full implementation is projected to reduce monthly operational expenditure (OpEx) by 31% ($240K annually) within six months, with zero degradation in service-level agreement (SLA) uptime.
```

### 4.3 Literatür Taraması İçin "Reporting Verbs" (Aktarım Fiilleri) Matrisi

Makalelerde sürekli *"Smith says that..."* veya *"Jones states that..."* demek yerine, yazarın tavrını (nötr, kanıtlayıcı, şüpheci, savunucu) yansıtan doğru fiili seçin:

| Yazarın Tavrı | C1-C2 Aktarım Fiilleri (Reporting Verbs) | Örnek Kullanım |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nötr / Açıklayıcı** | *posits, observes, notes, outlines, delineates, characterizes, documents* | *Chomsky (1965) **posits that** syntactic structures are innate.* |
| **Kanıtla Doğrulayan** | *demonstrates, establishes, corroborates, validates, substantiates, verifies, confirms* | *Recent neuroimaging studies **corroborate** the hypothesis that...* |
| **İddia Eden / Savunan** | *contends, maintains, asserts, argues, advocates, postulates, propounds* | *Kahneman (2011) **contends that** human decision-making is governed by two distinct systems.* |
| **Şüphe Duymak / Eleştirmek** | *challenges, refutes, calls into question, disputes, casts doubt on, critiques, rebuts* | *Subsequent meta-analyses **cast doubt on** the replicability of these early trials.* |
| **İhtiyatlı Öneri Sunan** | *hypothesizes, speculates, conjectures, implies, intimates, proposes* | *The authors **conjecture that** dark matter interactions may account for the anomaly.* |

## 🧪 Pratik: Paragraf Yeniden Yazım (B1 ➡️ C2 Transformation) ve Alıştırmalar

### Örnek Dönüşüm Analizi (Before & After)

> ❌ **B1/B2 Metni (Doğru ama Basit, Fiil Ağırlıklı, Kesin Yargılı):**
> *"Today, many companies use AI to make decisions about hiring workers. This makes the hiring process faster and cheaper. But it also causes big problems. If the old data has bias, the AI learns this bias and treats some groups unfairly. Because of this, governments must make strict rules so companies check their AI systems carefully before they use them."*

> ✅ **C1/C2 Akademik Metni (Nominalization + Hedging + Inversion + C1 Kelime Seçimi):**
> *"In contemporary human resource management, **the widespread adoption of** algorithmic decision-making has **streamlined** recruitment workflows while substantially reducing operational costs. **Not only does automation expedite** candidate screening, **but it also** introduces complex ethical **ramifications**. **Were historical training datasets to contain** embedded demographic biases, machine learning models **would inevitably perpetuate**—and potentially **exacerbate**—systemic discrimination. **Consequently**, **it is imperative that regulatory bodies mandate** rigorous algorithmic auditing **prior to** commercial deployment."*

**Kullanılan Tekniklerin Analizi:**
1. *many companies use AI* ➡️ **the widespread adoption of algorithmic decision-making** (Nominalization)
2. *makes faster + But it also* ➡️ **Not only does automation expedite..., but it also...** (Inversion + C1 Verb)
3. *If the old data has bias* ➡️ **Were historical training datasets to contain...** (Conditional Inversion)
4. *causes big problems / keeps bias* ➡️ **ethical ramifications / perpetuate and exacerbate** (C1 Lexis)
5. *governments must make strict rules* ➡️ **it is imperative that regulatory bodies mandate** (Subjunctive Mood)

### 📝 Kendini Sına: C1-C2 Dönüşüm Egzersizi

Aşağıdaki B1-B2 cümlelerini parantez içindeki C1-C2 tekniğini kullanarak yeniden yazın ve ardından cevabınızı kontrol edin:

1. **Soru 1 (Inversion - `Never before`):** *Scientists have never observed such a rapid collapse of an ice shelf.*
2. **Soru 2 (Subjunctive Mood - `imperative`):** *The security team must patch the vulnerability before Monday.*
3. **Soru 3 (Participle Clause - `Having + V3`):** *After the central bank had analyzed the inflation figures, it decided to hold interest rates steady.*
4. **Soru 4 (Nominalization + C1 Lexis):** *Because people did not trust the vaccine, the disease spread faster.*
5. **Soru 5 (Wh-Cleft + Hedging):** *This study shows that sleep quality affects memory more than sleep duration.*

<details>
<summary>🔑 <b>Cevap Anahtarını Görmek İçin Tıklayın</b></summary>

1. **Cevap 1:** ***Never before have scientists observed** such a rapid collapse of an ice shelf.*
2. **Cevap 2:** *It is **imperative that** the security team **patch** (veya: *that the vulnerability **be patched***) prior to Monday.*
3. **Cevap 3:** ***Having analyzed** the inflation figures, the central bank decided to maintain steady interest rates.*
4. **Cevap 4:** ***Public skepticism toward vaccination exacerbated the proliferation of** the pathogen.* (veya: *A deficit in public trust accelerated the transmission of the disease.*)
5. **Cevap 5:** ***What this study appears to indicate is that** sleep architecture exercises a more profound influence on memory consolidation than mere sleep duration.*

</details>
