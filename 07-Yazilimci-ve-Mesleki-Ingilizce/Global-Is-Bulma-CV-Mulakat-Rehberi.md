## 📄 ATS Uyumlu İngilizce CV (Resume) Hazırlama ve Google XYZ Formülü

Global şirketlere yapılan başvuruların %90'ı önce bir insan tarafından değil, **ATS (Applicant Tracking System)** adı verilen yazılımlar (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby) tarafından taranır.

### 1. Global CV (Resume) Altın Kuralları
* **Fotoğraf, Doğum Tarihi, Medeni Hal Koymayın:** ABD, İngiltere ve Kanada standartlarında CV'de fotoğraf, yaş veya medeni durum bulunması ayrımcılık yasaları (*anti-discrimination laws*) nedeniyle elenme sebebidir.
* **Tek Sütunlu, Sade Tasarım Kullanın:** Canva'daki çok sütunlu, grafikli, yüzdelik barlı (*"Java %85"*) şablonlar ATS botları tarafından okunamaz. Standart tek sütunlu (*Single-column*) format kullanın.
* **Görev Tanımı Değil, Ölçülebilir Etki (Impact) Yazın:** *"Worked on backend APIs"* veya *"Responsible for database"* gibi cümleler hiçbir şey ifade etmez.

## 🎯 İK (HR), Behavioral ve Teknik Mülakatlarda En Sık Sorulan 20 Soru ve STAR Cevapları

Global mülakatlarda *"Tell me about a time when..."* (Bana ... yaşadığın bir anı anlat) sorularına rastgele cevap verilmez. Tüm büyük teknoloji şirketleri (Amazon, Google, Meta, Stripe, Spotify) **STAR Tekniği** ile yapılandırılmış cevaplar bekler:

* **S — Situation (Durum - %15):** Bağlam neydi? Hangi şirket, hangi proje, nasıl bir ortam? (1-2 cümle)
* **T — Task (Görev / Sorun - %15):** Sizin çözmeniz gereken spesifik problem veya hedef neydi? (1-2 cümle)
* **A — Action (Aksiyon - %50):** **BİZ değil, BEN (`I`)** ne yaptım? Hangi teknik/iletişimsel adımları attım? (En önemli kısım!)
* **R — Result (Sonuç - %20):** Sayısal ve ölçülebilir sonuç ne oldu? Bundan ne öğrendiniz?

---

### BÖLÜM A: İK (HR Screening) ve Tanışma Soruları (Soru 1 – 5)

#### 1. *"Tell me about yourself."* (Bize kendinizden bahsedin.)
* 💡 **Strateji:** Hayat hikayenizi anlatmayın! **Present (Şu an) -> Past (Geçmiş) -> Future (Neden bu şirket?)** formülüyle 90 saniyede özetleyin.
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"Sure! I'm a Senior Full-Stack Engineer with over 5 years of experience specializing in TypeScript, React, and Node.js. **Currently**, at Acme Corp, I lead the core checkout squad where I architected a distributed payment pipeline handling over 100,000 daily transactions. **Previously**, I worked at a fast-growing B2B SaaS startup where I helped scale the platform from MVP to 50,000 users and introduced automated CI/CD workflows. **Right now**, I'm looking for my next challenge in a globally distributed product company where I can tackle high-scale reliability problems—which is why this role at [Company] immediately caught my eye."*

#### 2. *"Why are you looking to leave your current role?"* (Neden mevcut işinizden ayrılmak istiyorsunuz?)
* 💡 **Strateji:** Eski şirketinizi veya yöneticinizi **asla kötülemeyin**. "Kaçtığınızı" değil, "daha büyük bir hedefe doğru koştuğunuzu" (*pull factors, not push factors*) gösterin.
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"I've had a great run at my current company over the past three years and I'm really proud of what we built. However, the product has reached a very stable maintenance phase, and I thrive most when solving complex scaling challenges in a global, fast-paced environment. I'm looking to step into a role with broader architectural impact and international collaboration, which aligns closely with what your team is building."*

#### 3. *"Why do you want to work at [Company]?"* (Neden bizim şirketimizde çalışmak istiyorsunuz?)
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"Two main reasons: First, from a product perspective, you're solving a real pain point in developer tooling that I've personally experienced. Second, I read your engineering blog post on how you migrated to an event-driven architecture using Kafka, and I really admire your culture of pragmatic engineering and async-first remote work. It's the exact environment where I can both contribute from day one and keep growing."*

#### 4. *"What is your experience working in remote / asynchronous teams?"* (Uzaktan ve asenkron çalışma deneyiminiz nedir?)
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"Over the past three years, I've worked in fully remote teams spanning three time zones. I'm a strong believer in over-communicating through clear documentation—whether that's writing detailed PR descriptions, recording short Loom videos to explain UI changes, or documenting architectural decisions in Notion so teammates in other time zones are never blocked waiting for me to wake up."*

#### 5. *"What is your greatest weakness?"* (En büyük zayıf yönünüz nedir?)
* 💡 **Strateji:** *"Çok mükemmeliyetçiyim"* gibi klişe cevaplardan kaçının. Gerçek ama ölümcül olmayan bir alışkanlık ve onu **nasıl sistemle çözdüğünüzü** anlatın.
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"Early in my career, I had a tendency to dive too deep into optimizing code or solving a tricky bug on my own for hours before asking for help—what we call yak shaving. To fix this, I adopted a strict '45-minute timebox rule': if I'm stuck on an environment or domain-specific issue for 45 minutes with no progress, I write down what I've tried and ask a teammate in Slack. That habit has drastically improved my delivery speed."*

### BÖLÜM B: Davranışsal (Behavioral - STAR) Sorular (Soru 6 – 13)

#### 6. *"Tell me about a time you disagreed with a teammate or senior engineer on a technical decision."* (Teknik bir kararda ekip arkadaşınızla fikir ayrılığı yaşadığınız bir anı anlatın.)
* 🗣️ **STAR Cevabı:**
  > * **S/T:** *"At my previous company, we were designing a new notification service. A senior engineer proposed building a custom polling mechanism on top of PostgreSQL, whereas I believed using Redis Streams or RabbitMQ would prevent database lock contention."*
  > * **A:** *"Instead of turning it into an opinion battle, I proposed a quick 2-hour benchmark POC. I spun up a load test simulating 5,000 concurrent users against both approaches and shared the CPU and latency graphs in a short RFC document."*
  > * **R:** *"The data clearly showed PostgreSQL CPU spiking to 90% under polling. The senior engineer appreciated the data-driven approach, we went with Redis Streams, and the service has run smoothly in production for over a year with sub-10ms latency."*

#### 7. *"Describe a production incident or bug you caused and how you handled it."* (Canlı ortamda sebep olduğunuz bir hatayı ve nasıl yönettiğinizi anlatın.)
* 🗣️ **STAR Cevabı:**
  > * **S/T:** *"Last year, I deployed a database migration that added a `NOT NULL` constraint with a default value on a 20-million-row table during business hours, which acquired an `ACCESS EXCLUSIVE` lock and caused API timeouts."*
  > * **A:** *"Within two minutes, our Datadog alert fired. I immediately owned up to it in the `#incidents` channel, cancelled the blocking query, and rolled back the deployment—restoring service in under 6 minutes. Afterwards, I wrote a blameless post-mortem."*
  > * **R:** *"To make sure nobody could repeat my mistake, I integrated `squawk`—an automated PostgreSQL migration linter—into our GitHub Actions CI pipeline to block unsafe locking migrations before they ever get merged."*

#### 8. *"Tell me about a time you had to deal with tight deadlines or changing requirements."* (Sıkışık bir teslim tarihi veya değişen gereksinimlerle başa çıktığınız bir durumu anlatın.)
* 🗣️ **STAR Cevabı:**
  > * **S/T:** *"Two weeks before a major enterprise client launch, the client requested custom SSO (SAML) integration, which wasn't in our original sprint scope."*
  > * **A:** *"I immediately sat down with our Product Manager to evaluate the trade-offs. Rather than saying a flat 'no' or working 80-hour weeks and shipping buggy code, I proposed scoping an MVP: we would use Auth0's enterprise SAML connector for the launch and defer the self-serve admin UI by one sprint."*
  > * **R:** *"The PM and client agreed. We launched on time with zero security issues, closed the $120k annual contract, and delivered the self-serve UI two weeks later."*

#### 9. *"Tell me about a time you improved a slow system or paid down significant technical debt."* (Yavaş bir sistemi hızlandırdığınız veya teknik borç temizlediğiniz bir anı anlatın.)
* 🗣️ **STAR Cevabı:**
  > * **S/T:** *"Our customer CSV export endpoint was timing out whenever clients tried exporting more than 50,000 rows, generating 15+ support tickets a week."*
  > * **A:** *"I profiled the endpoint and discovered it was loading all ORM entities into memory at once and serializing them synchronously. I refactored the endpoint to use database cursor streaming combined with an asynchronous background worker that uploads the file to S3 and emails a signed download link."*
  > * **R:** *"Memory usage dropped by 85%, exports of 1 million+ rows began completing reliably without HTTP timeouts, and related support tickets dropped to zero."*

#### 10. *"Describe a situation where you had to work with unclear or ambiguous requirements."* (Belirsiz gereksinimlerle çalışmak zorunda kaldığınız bir durumu anlatın.)
* 🗣️ **STAR Cevabı:**
  > * **S/T:** *"I was asked to build a 'Fraud Detection Alert System' with only a two-sentence Jira ticket and no formal product spec."*
  > * **A:** *"Instead of guessing and building the wrong thing, I wrote a 1-page RFC outlining three concrete questions: what constitutes a fraud trigger, what false-positive rate is acceptable, and who receives the alert. I scheduled a 20-minute sync with Risk and Product to lock down the answers."*
  > * **R:** *"That 20-minute alignment saved us at least two weeks of wasted engineering effort, and we shipped a rule-based MVP within a single sprint."*

#### 11. *"Tell me about a time you mentored someone or helped a teammate grow."* (Birine mentorluk yaptığınız veya gelişimine katkıda bulunduğunuz bir anı anlatın.)
* 🗣️ **STAR Cevabı:**
  > * **S/T:** *"When a new junior developer joined our remote team, they were hesitant to open PRs and took nearly a week on their first small ticket."*
  > * **A:** *"I reached out proactively and set up three 30-minute pair programming sessions that week. We walked through the codebase architecture together, and I documented an 'Onboarding Cheat Sheet' in Notion covering our local setup and testing conventions."*
  > * **R:** *"Within three weeks, that developer was shipping features independently, and our Onboarding Cheat Sheet reduced ramp-up time for future hires by 40%."*

#### 12. *"Tell me about a time you received critical feedback. How did you respond?"* (Aldığınız eleştirel bir geri bildirimi ve nasıl tepki verdiğinizi anlatın.)
* 🗣️ **STAR Cevabı:**
  > * **S/T:** *"In a mid-year review, my Engineering Manager noted that while my code quality was high, my PRs were often too large (800+ lines), making them slow and exhausting for teammates to review."*
  > * **A:** *"I took the feedback constructively and changed my workflow immediately. I started using feature flags and stacked PRs, keeping each PR focused on a single logical layer under 250 lines of code."*
  > * **R:** *"My average PR review turnaround time dropped from 2 days to under 4 hours, and several teammates thanked me in our next retrospective."*

#### 13. *"What is the most complex technical project you've ever worked on?"* (Şimdiye kadar üzerinde çalıştığınız en karmaşık teknik proje nedir?)
* 🗣️ **STAR Cevabı:**
  > * **S/T:** *"The most complex project I led was migrating our multi-tenant SaaS database from a single shared PostgreSQL instance to a sharded architecture without any scheduled downtime."*
  > * **A:** *"I designed a 4-phase dual-write migration strategy: first writing to both old and new shards, then running a background backfill worker with checksum verification, next switching read traffic via feature flags per tenant, and finally decommissioning the old tables."*
  > * **R:** *"We migrated 1.2 TB of live customer data with zero data loss and zero customer-facing downtime, unlocking 10x horizontal write scalability."*

### BÖLÜM C: Teknik Derinlik, Mimari ve Süreç Soruları (Soru 14 – 20)

#### 14. *"How do you ensure code quality and prevent bugs before production?"* (Kod kalitesini nasıl sağlarsınız ve canlıya çıkmadan hataları nasıl önlersiniz?)
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"I rely on a layered 'Testing Trophy' approach rather than manual QA alone: strict static typing with TypeScript, unit tests for pure business logic, integration tests against real database containers (using Testcontainers) for API routes, and automated linting/security checks in CI. Beyond tests, small PRs, thorough code reviews, and gradual rollouts using feature flags and canary deployments are my biggest safeguards."*

#### 15. *"How do you approach debugging an issue in production that you cannot reproduce locally?"* (Lokal ortamda tekrar üretemediğiniz bir canlı ortam hatasını nasıl çözersiniz?)
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"First, I check observability tools—structured logs, distributed traces (OpenTelemetry/Datadog), and error tracking (Sentry)—correlating the exact timestamp, tenant ID, and request payload. Often non-reproducible bugs stem from race conditions, data-shape anomalies in production, or environment config drift. If logs aren't enough, I add targeted, non-intrusive debug logging behind a feature flag for that specific tenant ID and write a failing integration test using the sanitized production payload shape."*

#### 16. *"How do you balance shipping fast vs. writing clean, maintainable code?"* (Hızlı ürün çıkarmak ile temiz/sürdürülebilir kod yazmak arasındaki dengeyi nasıl kurarsınız?)
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"I view code quality through the lens of risk and reversibility. If we're building a short-lived experiment to validate product-market fit, I'm happy to take pragmatic shortcuts—as long as we isolate them behind clean interfaces and document the tech debt in backlog tickets. However, on core domains like authentication, billing, or data integrity, cutting corners actually slows you down within weeks, so I insist on high test coverage and solid design upfront."*

#### 17. *"Walk me through what happens when you type `https://google.com` into a browser and press Enter."* (Klasik Sistem/Ağ Mülakat Sorusu)
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"In short: 1) The browser checks its local DNS cache, OS cache, and router, then queries a recursive DNS resolver to translate `google.com` into an IP address. 2) It establishes a TCP connection with the server via a 3-way handshake (`SYN`, `SYN-ACK`, `ACK`). 3) For HTTPS, it performs a TLS handshake to verify the SSL certificate and agree on symmetric encryption keys. 4) The browser sends an HTTP `GET` request through the encrypted tunnel, hitting Google's edge Load Balancer / CDN. 5) The server responds with an `HTTP 200 OK` and the HTML payload. 6) Finally, the browser parses the HTML into the DOM, CSS into the CSSOM, builds the Render Tree, executes JavaScript, and paints the pixels on the screen."*

#### 18. *"When would you choose SQL (Relational) vs. NoSQL (Non-Relational) databases?"* (SQL ve NoSQL tercihini neye göre yaparsınız?)
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"PostgreSQL (relational) is my default choice for 90% of applications because most business domains have structured relationships, require ACID transactions, and benefit from strong consistency. I reach for NoSQL—such as DynamoDB, Cassandra, or MongoDB—when we need massive horizontal write throughput with simple key-value access patterns, ultra-low latency at global scale, or genuinely unstructured/polymorphic document schemas."*

#### 19. *"How do you keep your technical skills up to date?"* (Teknik becerilerinizi nasıl güncel tutarsınız?)
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"I combine passive curating with active building. I read engineering blogs like ByteByteGo and The Pragmatic Engineer, follow RFCs and changelogs of our core stack, and read source code of well-written open-source libraries on GitHub. Whenever a new tool solves a real problem I have, I build a small weekend proof-of-concept to test its rough edges rather than just watching tutorials."*

#### 20. *"Where do you see yourself in 3 to 5 years?"* (3-5 yıl içinde kendinizi nerede görüyorsunuz?)
* 🗣️ **Örnek Cevap:**
  > *"My goal is to grow along the Individual Contributor (IC) track toward a Staff Engineer or Tech Lead role. I love staying hands-on with code and architecture while multiplying the output of the people around me—leading cross-team system design initiatives, mentoring engineers, and helping align technical strategy with business goals."*

## ❓ Mülakat Sonunda Sizin Sormanız Gereken "Ters Köşe" Sorular

Mülakatın son 5 dakikasında *"Do you have any questions for us?"* dendiğinde **"No, everything is clear"** demek büyük bir eksi puandır. Sizi kıdemli ve seçici gösteren sorular şunlardır:

### Teknik Ekibe / Mühendislere Sorulacak Sorular
1. *"What does the day-to-day engineering workflow look like here—from picking up a ticket to deploying it to production?"*
2. *"What is the biggest technical challenge or scaling bottleneck the team is planning to tackle over the next 6 months?"*
3. *"How does the team handle on-call rotations, production incidents, and paying down technical debt?"*

### Hiring Manager / CTO'ya Sorulacak Sorular
4. *"If I join the team, what would a successful first 90 days look like in your eyes?"*
5. *"How are product roadmap decisions made between Product Management and Engineering?"*

## 💰 Maaş ve Kontrat Müzakeresi (Salary Negotiation) Konuşma ve E-posta Şablonları

Global şirketler ilk tekliflerinde (*Initial Offer*) her zaman **%10 ile %25 arasında bir müzakere payı (buffer)** bırakırlar. Profesyonelce müzakere yapmak teklifin geri çekilmesine **neden olmaz**; aksine kıdemli duruşunuzu kanıtlar.

### Senaryo 1: İlk İK Görüşmesinde "Maaş Beklentiniz Nedir?" Sorusunu Savuşturma
İlk görüşmede sayı verirseniz ya çok düşük kalıp para kaybedersiniz ya da bütçe üstü kalıp elenirsiniz. Topu karşı tarafa atın (*Reverse the Question*):

> **İK (Recruiter):** *"What are your salary expectations for this role?"*
>
> **Sizin Cevabınız (Canlı Konuşma):**
> *"Right now, my main priority is finding the right technical fit and learning more about the scope of the role. Since you know the level and internal bands for this position much better than I do, **could you share the approved compensation range budgeted for this role?**"*
>
> *(Eğer ısrarla bir sayı isterlerse):*
> *"Based on my research of the global remote market for Senior Engineers with my experience, I'm generally looking in the range of **$X,000 to $Y,000 base**, depending on the overall compensation package—including bonus, equity, and PTO. Does that align with your budget?"*

---

### Senaryo 2: Teklif Geldikten Sonra Canlı Telefonda İlk Tepki (Asla Anında "Evet" Demeyin!)
Teklif telefonda söylendiğinde heyecanlanıp hemen kabul etmeyin; düşünmek için yazılı teklifi isteyin:

> *"Thank you so much, [Name]! I'm really excited about the team and the technical challenges we discussed during the interviews. I'd love to review the full details of the offer in writing and discuss it with my family. **Could you send over the written offer breakdown**, and can we reconnect on [Day, e.g., Thursday]?"*

---

### Senaryo 3: Maaş Artışı İsteme E-postası (Counter-Offer Email Template)
Bu şablon, şimdiye kadar yüzlerce mühendisin ilk teklifini **$5,000 – $25,000+** yukarı çekmesini sağlamış diplomatik ve veriye dayalı şablondur:

```text
Subject: Re: Offer from [Company Name] — [Your Full Name]

Hi [Recruiter Name],

Thank you again for extending the offer for the [Role Title] position! I really enjoyed meeting [Hiring Manager Name] and the engineering team, and I'm genuinely excited about the opportunity to contribute to [Specific Project / Team Goal discussed in interviews].

After carefully reviewing the compensation package, I'd love to discuss the base salary component. Given my [X+ years] of experience with [Core Tech Stack], my track record of [1 key achievement, e.g., scaling high-throughput payment systems], and current market rates for similar senior remote roles, I was hoping we could bring the base salary closer to [$Target Amount, e.g., $95,000 / €80,000].

If we can bridge that gap and get the base to [$Target Amount] (or supplement the difference with a [$X,000] sign-on bonus), I would be thrilled to sign the offer letter today and wrap up my other ongoing interview processes immediately.

Thank you so much for advocating for me with the hiring team—I look forward to hearing your thoughts!

Best regards,
[Your Name]
```

---

### Senaryo 4: Yurt Dışı B2B Contractor (Sözleşmeli / Faturalı) Çalışmada Ek Hakları Müzakere Etme
Yurt dışındaki şirketlerin çoğu Türkiye'deki yazılımcıları **B2B Independent Contractor** (Deel, Remote.com, Oyster üzerinden şahıs/limited şirketiyle fatura keserek) işe alır. Maaş tavanına ulaştıysanız şu 4 kalemi müzakere edin:

| Müzakere Kalemi | Nasıl İstenir? (İngilizce Kalıp) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Ücretli İzin (Paid Time Off - PTO)** | *"Since this is a B2B contractor agreement, could we include **25 days of paid time off plus local public holidays** in the contract?"* |
| **2. Donanım & Ev Ofis Bütçesi (Hardware Stipend)** | *"Does the company provide a **MacBook Pro / hardware stipend** (e.g., $2,500) and an annual **home-office / co-working budget**?"* |
| **3. İmza Bonusu (Sign-on Bonus)** | *"I understand the base salary is capped at $X due to internal leveling bands. Would it be possible to include a **one-time $5,000 sign-on bonus** to make the transition seamless?"* |
| **4. Erken Maaş Gözden Geçirmesi (6-Month Review)** | *"If we start at $X today, could we put in writing a **performance and compensation review at the 6-month mark** to adjust the rate to $Y upon meeting my onboarding milestones?"* |
